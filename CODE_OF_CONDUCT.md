# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a precise, useful and welcoming engineering community. Be respectful, critique ideas rather than people, share relevant context, and make space for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate exposure of private information, spam, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on community safety.

Report conduct concerns privately to `amir@amirkazemkhani.com`. Reports will be handled confidentially and acknowledged within 72 hours where practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
